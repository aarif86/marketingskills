The Water Cycle: quick quiz

5 questions, about 3 minutes. Do it on your own first, then we go through the answers in class on Thursday. From Mrs Tan.

1. Water turning from liquid into water vapour is called…

Evaporation: liquid water gains heat and becomes water vapour. Condensation is the opposite.

2. Why do the outside of a cold Milo cup get wet in the canteen?

The cup cools the air next to it, so water vapour in the air loses heat and condenses into droplets.

3. Which of these makes evaporation faster?

Wind, higher temperature and a larger exposed surface all speed up evaporation.

4. Clouds are made of…

Water vapour is invisible. Clouds are the droplets and ice crystals formed when vapour condenses high up.

5. Singapore has rain almost every week. Where does most of that water come from?

We are surrounded by warm sea. Water evaporates from it, condenses into clouds, and falls as rain.
